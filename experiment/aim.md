### Learning Objectives

1. To understand the basics of Complete Blood Count (CBC) testing used in clinical settings.

2. To study the identification of blood cells using staining techniques. 

3. To understand the basic principles and functions of automated haematology analysers used in CBC testing.

4. To calculate and interpret red-cell indices (MCV, MCH, and MCHC) using CBC parameters.

5. To analyse and evaluate CBC results to identify abnormalities and recognize different types of anaemia.
